# Finding Prioritization

Severity and priority are different concepts.

Severity describes how serious an individual problem is.

Priority describes which problem should be addressed first.

---

## Priority Factors

The agent should consider:

### 1. Blocking impact

Does the issue prevent automated systems from accessing or discovering
important information?

### 2. Business importance

Does the issue affect:

- primary products
- important landing pages
- conversion paths
- core organization information
- important content

### 3. Scope

Does the issue affect:

- one page
- multiple pages
- the entire website

### 4. Evidence confidence

Prioritize findings supported by strong, direct evidence over weak
signals.

### 5. Dependency

An issue that causes several other problems may deserve higher priority.

For example:

A rendering problem may cause missing content, missing structured
data, and poor discoverability.

---

## Priority Rules

Priority should be assigned after all skills have completed.

The agent should:

1. Review all findings together.
2. Identify blocking issues.
3. Identify high-impact issues.
4. Consider scope and business importance.
5. Order findings from most urgent to least urgent.
6. Give each priority a short reason.

Do not simply sort by severity.

A medium-severity issue affecting the entire website may deserve a
higher priority than a high-severity issue affecting one unimportant
page.
