# Engagement Severity Guidance

## Critical

Use only where an important conversion or information path is
effectively inaccessible or unusable.

## High

Use where important actions or information require substantial
interaction or are poorly exposed to automated systems.

## Medium

Use for meaningful discoverability or usability weaknesses.

Examples:

- unclear CTA labels
- missing descriptive link context
- weak content hierarchy
- important information hidden behind interaction

## Low

Use for minor optimization opportunities.

Severity should consider:

1. Importance of the action/content.
2. Whether the action is completely blocked or merely less clear.
3. Whether alternate access paths exist.
4. Evidence from multiple checks.
