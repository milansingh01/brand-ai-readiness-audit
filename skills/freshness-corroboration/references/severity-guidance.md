# Freshness and Corroboration Severity Guidance

## Critical

Use only when an important contradiction can materially misrepresent
the entity or information being surfaced.

## High

Use when important information appears significantly stale or when
strong contradictory signals exist.

## Medium

Use for meaningful missing freshness signals or moderate inconsistencies.

## Low

Use for minor metadata gaps or weak freshness signals.

Do not infer that content is factually wrong solely because it is old.

Consider:

1. Content type.
2. Detected age.
3. Whether the information is time-sensitive.
4. Whether multiple sources agree.
5. Importance of the affected information.
